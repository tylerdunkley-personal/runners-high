RUNNER’S HIGH / OS / VERSION 1.0

LOUD. LOCAL. ONLINE.

Runner’s High is a band from Jacksonville, Florida. Their Bandcamp catalog spans Update Me (2017), Gone Fishin’ (2020), GRASS-FED (2021), and 2026 singles.

Open the folders. Play something loud. Beat your high score. Stay a while.

HOW TO USE THIS THING

Click a desktop icon to open an app. Drag a title bar to move a window. Use the taskbar to bring it back.

rhTunes: THE BAND plays “Rat Motel” through the official Bandcamp embed. MY MIXTAPE lets you load local audio; files stay on your device. Pause the Bandcamp player before starting the arcade.

RAT ARCADE: four lanes. A / S / K / L, or tap the colored fret buttons. Hit notes at the target line. The original practice riff is separate from the band’s music. Adjust TIMING if your audio setup feels late.

The xerox files: three pages of releases, recording credits, and links, sourced from the band’s Bandcamp.
